`@nib-ui/css` adds form fields: `nib-input` (input, textarea, select), `nib-choice` (checkbox and radio printing `[x]` / `(•)` glyphs over a native input), `nib-switch` (`role="switch"`, knob-less `ON` / `OFF`), `nib-field` with label, `OPTIONAL` marker, hint and error, `nib-fieldset`, and `nib-error-summary`. States come from `aria-invalid`, `:checked`, `:indeterminate` and `:disabled`; rows are 44px tall on touch.
