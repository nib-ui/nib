`@nib-ui/css` gains its base layer: reset, type role classes (`nib-text-*`, `nib-kicker`, `nib-num`), `nib-prose` at the 68ch reading width, `nib-container`, the ruled `nib-grid`, `nib-with-side`, `nib-pane-header`, the `nib-skip` link and a 2px accent focus ring. Caps come from `--nib-caps` and switch off for scripts without case; any region can set `data-theme` and repaints its own ground.
