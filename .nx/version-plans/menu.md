Menu button: `createMenu` in `@nib-ui/core/menu` (APG menu button on a native popover; items, checkbox and radio items, arrows, Home, End, typeahead, Enter/Space choose and close, Tab closes), `useMenu` (React, Vue), `createMenuStore` (Svelte), `data-nib="menu"` (vanilla, with `nib:select`), and `nib-menu` / `__item` / `__separator` in `@nib-ui/css`. Core gains a `popover` intent (show or hide a native popover), run by `@nib-ui/dom`; `place()` gains `measurePlacement` and `samePlacement` helpers.
