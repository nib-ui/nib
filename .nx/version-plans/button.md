`@nib-ui/css` adds the button (`nib-button` with `--primary`, `--outline`, `--ghost`, `--destructive`, `--lg`, the printed shortcut `nib-button__key`) and the `nib-action-row` it sits in. States come from `aria-pressed` (cursor block), `disabled` and `aria-disabled`; forced colours and right-to-left are covered.
